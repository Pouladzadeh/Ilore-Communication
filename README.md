Ilore Communication: AI-Augmented AAC Platform"Transforming every child's unique sounds into confidence and connection." 📌 Strategic OverviewIlore is a next-generation Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) platform designed to solve the critical communication gap for the 25-30% of autistic children who are minimally verbal or nonverbal.While traditional assistive devices often cost between $1,500 and $15,000, they frequently fail to adapt to a child's specific needs. Ilore disrupts this space by combining high-fidelity visual communication with adaptive AI to create an affordable, intuitive, and emotionally resonant solution.🛠️ The Senior Approach: Design & EngineeringThis project represents a full-cycle product development process, moving from deep user research to a functional, "vibe-coded" prototype.1. AI Design ThinkingInstead of building a static icon board, I designed an adaptive ecosystem:AI-Powered Voice Recognition: A system designed to learn a child’s unique vocal patterns and "translate" them into meaningful speech in real-time.Visual Communication System: A low-cognitive-load interface using high-contrast picture cards to facilitate immediate expression.Engagement-First UX: Integration of a personalized avatar to foster emotional connection and reduce the "clinical" feel of traditional AAC tools.2. Rapid Prototyping via "Vibe Coding"To validate the user experience beyond static mocks, I utilized an LLM-assisted development workflow (vibe coding) to build a functional front-end demo:Stack: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript.Efficiency: This approach allowed for rapid iteration of complex interaction patterns, ensuring the "feel" of the communication grid was fluid and responsive before moving into full-scale production.Live Demo: View the Functional Prototype📈 Market & Impact StrategyIlore is built with scalability in mind, targeting a diverse ecosystem of stakeholders:Families: Reducing the $60,000 annual average burden of therapies and assistive tools.Education: Breaking down barriers for the 70% of nonverbal children placed in restrictive classrooms due to communication gaps.Clinical: Partnering with speech and behavioral specialists to embed Ilore into standardized care plans.🚀 Current Status & Roadmap (2026)The design and front-end foundation are complete.
